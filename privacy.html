Privacy

Forgor holds dates. It is built so that the smallest possible amount of anything else passes through it.

What we keep

The dates you keep, and what you told us about them: a name, a kind, a day, whether it repeats, how far ahead you want the nudge, and any note you write. Your household, and who is in it. Your name, an email address and a phone number for your account, so that you can sign in with a code and your household can find you.

Your contacts and your photos

When you bring in birthdays from your contacts, Forgor first tells you what happens and asks you to agree, then asks iOS for Contacts access. It reads only each contact's name, birthday and anniversary fields, on your phone. You then see the list and untick anyone. For each person you choose to keep, their name and that date are uploaded to your Forgor account on our server — that is how your list syncs across your devices, reaches the household members you share it with, and produces your reminders. Contacts you don't keep never leave your phone, and nothing else on a contact card (numbers, emails, addresses, photos, notes) is read. Names and dates from your contacts are never sold, used for advertising, or used to contact anyone; deleting the date, or your account, removes them from our server. When you scan a photo, the text is read on your phone and the image is never uploaded — only the date you choose to keep is saved.

Mail you forward

Each household gets its own forwarding address. Mail sent there is read by an AI model (Anthropic’s Claude) to find the date in it, and the mail itself is not stored. What is kept is the date itself and an excerpt of at most 140 characters, so you can see where the date came from. Mail from an address that is not yours is dropped without being read.

What we do not do

There is no advertising in Forgor, and nothing about you is sold or shared for marketing. Your dates are not used to train anyone's model.

Who else touches the data

Forgor runs on a small number of providers that handle sign-in, storage, mail delivery and the date extraction above. They process the data on Forgor's instruction and for no purpose of their own.

Deleting it

You can delete any date at any time. To delete your account, go to Settings → Account → Delete account in the app: your dates, your membership of the household and your account are removed, and if you were the only member the household goes with them. Prefer to ask us instead? Write to hello@forgor.app.

Children

Forgor is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so rather than quietly updating this page.
