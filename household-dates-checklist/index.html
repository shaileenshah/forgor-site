forgor Household checklist

Every date your household should be tracking.

People, documents, the car, the home and money: the dates that come back, and how often.

Every household runs on dates that come back: renewals, expirations, birthdays, the filter nobody remembers. Most cost money, or feelings, when they slip. Here's the full list, by category, with how often each one comes around. Checked October 2026.

People

Every year

Birthdays and anniversaries

Friends, parents, in-laws, nieces and nephews, and your own anniversary. See how to remember birthdays for a setup that works.

Every year

The holidays your family keeps

Mother's Day is Sunday, May 9, 2027 and Father's Day is Sunday, June 20, 2027. Add the ones your family celebrates, with enough lead time to plan.

Documents

Every 10 years (adults), 5 years (kids under 16)

Passports

Kids' passports expire first and can't be renewed by mail. Many countries want months of validity left when you arrive. Check every passport in the house.

On the date printed on the card

Driver's licenses and IDs

Look at each card in the house and write the expiry down. Most people find out at a rental counter or the airport.

The car

Every year

Registration

In Texas you can renew up to 90 days early, and emissions testing comes first in DFW counties. See your renewal window.

Every 6 or 12 months

Car insurance renewal

The month the premium tends to change. Worth a reminder a few weeks ahead so you can compare.

The home

Check monthly, change at least every 3 months

HVAC air filter

ENERGY STAR's rule of thumb: check it every month and change it at least every three months.

Test monthly, batteries yearly

Smoke alarms

Test them monthly, replace batteries at least once a year if they aren't sealed 10-year units, and replace the alarms themselves every 10 years.

Texas homeowners

Homestead exemption and appraisal protest

File the homestead exemption once (and answer any re-check letter); watch for the appraisal notice each spring. The Texas homeowner checklist has the full house calendar.

Money

When the trial ends

Trials and subscriptions

The day a trial turns into a charge, and the annual renewals that bill once a year. Find the exact bill date.

When it runs out

Warranties and return windows

The return-by date on anything still in the box, and the warranty end date on anything that might break.

Make it one list

Put every date above in one place the whole house can see, with one nudge ahead of each. That's the whole idea behind Forgor: no to-do pile, nothing ever overdue, and each date rolls forward on its own. Not sure where you stand? Take the 60-second test.

General guidance, not professional advice. Sources: ENERGY STAR (air filters); American Red Cross and NFPA guidance (smoke alarms); U.S. Department of State (passports); TxDMV (registration).
