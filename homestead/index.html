When is my homestead exemption due?

Bought a home in Texas? The homestead exemption takes $140,000 off your home's value for school taxes. It isn't automatic. Find your deadline and where to file.

A guide, not tax advice. Confirm the details with your appraisal district. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

The short version

  • File with your county appraisal district, usually online. Most districts take Form 50-114 with a copy of your Texas driver's license showing the home's address.
  • The deadline for each tax year is before May 1 (April 30). If you buy after January 1, you can still get it for the part of the year you owned the home.
  • Missed it? You can file late, up to two years after that year's taxes were due.
  • School districts must exempt $140,000; there's an extra $60,000 if you're 65 or older or disabled.
  • You don't refile every year, but your appraisal district must re-check eligibility at least every five years. If a letter comes, answer it by its deadline or the exemption can be removed.
  • Think your value is too high? The protest deadline is May 15, or 30 days after your notice is mailed, whichever is later.

Sources: Texas Comptroller, property tax exemptions; Texas Tax Code §11.431 (late applications). Checked October 2026.
