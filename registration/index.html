When can I renew my registration?

Pick the month on your windshield sticker. See when the renewal window opens, when to get the emissions test, and the last safe day.

A guide for non-commercial vehicles. Confirm with TxDMV or your county tax office. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

The short version

  • You can renew up to 90 days before your registration expires, online, by mail or at your county tax office.
  • Since January 1, 2025, non-commercial vehicles no longer need a safety inspection. A $7.50 inspection program fee is added at registration instead.
  • An emissions test is still required before you renew in 17 counties, including Collin, Dallas, Denton, Ellis, Johnson, Kaufman, Parker, Rockwall and Tarrant. You have a 90-day window to pass it and register.
  • After it expires there are 5 working days of grace; after that you can be cited.

Sources: Texas DPS, inspection program changes; Tarrant County, Two Steps, One Sticker; TxDMV. Checked October 2026.
