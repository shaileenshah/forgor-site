Bring your important dates in from your calendar.

Forgor reads the calendars on your iPhone (iCloud, Google, Outlook), finds the birthdays, renewals and appointments worth a nudge, and lets you keep the ones you want. It never changes your calendar.

iPhone calendars · available now iCloud · Google · Outlook Read-only
How it works

Switch on, choose, keep.

  1. 1

    Switch on your calendars

    In Settings → Sources → iPhone calendars. Your iPhone asks once whether Forgor may read them.

  2. 2

    Choose which ones

    Switch on Family and Birthdays, leave Work off. Holiday calendars stay out of the way.

  3. 3

    Keep what matters

    Forgor suggests the dates worth a nudge. Keep one, keep them all, or skip. Nothing is added without you.

What it looks for

The dates that come back, not every meeting.

Birthdays and anniversaries, renewals, bills, appointments, trips. Standups and lunch don't make the list.

If you already saved a date, or it sits in two calendars, it shows once. If an event moves or is cancelled later, Forgor shows a card so you can update yours or keep it.

Questions

Can Forgor import birthdays from my calendar?

Yes. Switch on the calendars that hold birthdays and Forgor suggests each one, repeating every year, with a nudge ahead of time.

Does it work with Google Calendar and Outlook?

Yes, through your iPhone. If your Google or Outlook account is added to the iPhone's Calendar app, Forgor reads those calendars on the phone, the same way it reads iCloud.

How do I bring in my Outlook calendar?

A personal Outlook account (outlook.com, hotmail.com, live.com or msn.com) works through your iPhone calendars:

  1. On your iPhone, add the account under Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts, with Calendars turned on.
  2. In Forgor, open Sources → iPhone calendars. Your Outlook calendars show under an “Outlook” heading, with the email address beside it.
  3. Switch on the ones you want and tap Turn on.
Will Forgor change or delete my events?

No. Access is read-only.

What is sent off my phone?

Only if you agree: the titles, dates and times of events in the calendars you switched on go to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude), only to pick out the dates worth a reminder. They are not used to train a model. Details are in the privacy policy.

What happens when I add a new event later?

Forgor checks again when you open the app or pull down to refresh, and suggests anything new. If an event you kept moves or is cancelled, you'll see a card.

Your calendar has the dates. Forgor has the nudge.
