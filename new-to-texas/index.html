Just moved to Texas? Here are the deadlines.

Car registration in 30 days, a Texas license in 90, emissions tests in DFW, and the homestead exemption. What to do, and when.

Moving brings a pile of paperwork with clocks attached. Here are the Texas ones, in the order they come due, with links to the official pages. Checked October 2026.

Your car

Within 30 days of moving

Register your vehicle in Texas

You have 30 days from the time you move to register your vehicle. Go to your county tax assessor-collector office with proof of insurance, proof of ownership and Form 130-U (Application for Texas Title and/or Registration). The base registration fee is $50.75 plus $1 for TexasSure; counties add their own fees, and a new-resident tax may apply. TxDMV: New to Texas

Before you register

Pass an emissions test, if your county requires one

In DFW, Collin, Dallas, Denton, Ellis, Johnson, Kaufman, Parker, Rockwall and Tarrant counties require a passing emissions inspection before registration. Safety inspections for non-commercial vehicles ended January 1, 2025.

Before you register

Meet Texas minimum insurance

At least $30,000 per injured person, $60,000 per accident for injuries, and $25,000 for property damage.

Every year after

Renew on time

Texas lets you renew up to 90 days early. Use the registration window calculator to see your dates, and add the expiry to Forgor so it never sneaks up.

Your license

Within 90 days of moving

Get a Texas driver license

You can drive on a valid license from another state for up to 90 days after you move. To switch, bring proof of identity, Texas residency, citizenship or lawful presence, your Social Security number, and Texas registration and insurance for each vehicle you own. Register the car first. Texas DPS: Moving to Texas

Your home

By April 30

File the homestead exemption, if you bought

It takes $140,000 off your home's value for school taxes, and it isn't automatic. If you buy after January 1, you can still get it for the part of the year you owned the home. Find your deadline and appraisal district with the homestead calculator.

Each spring

Check your appraisal notice

If the value looks high, the protest deadline is May 15, or 30 days after the notice is mailed, whichever is later.

The ones that repeat

Most of these come back every year: registration, the appraisal notice, insurance renewals, the passport you'll need for the next trip. A calendar entry gets buried; a to-do app makes them pile up and go red. Forgor keeps them on one household list, nudges the right person once, early, and rolls each date forward on its own.
