The whole product, in one line

A nudge two weeks out, then silence.

Forgor tells you once, early enough that you can still do something about it, and then gets out of the way. No badge on the icon, no daily digest, no second reminder about the first reminder.

You choose the hour it arrives, and how far ahead — two weeks unless you say otherwise; passports get two months, haircuts get three days. Everything else is already decided.
