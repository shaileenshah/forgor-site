forgor Birthdays

Never miss a birthday again.

Why birthdays slip, and a ten-minute setup that gets the card out on time, for every friend, parent, niece and in-law.

Nobody forgets a birthday on purpose. It slips because the date lives in one place (a social app, a phone contact, someone else's head) and the reminder, if there is one, shows up on the day, when it's too late for a card or a gift. The fix isn't a better memory. It's a better setup.

Why the usual ways fail

Social media

It tells you on the day, if at all

Fine for a "happy birthday" comment. Useless for anything that needs to arrive, and it only knows the people who are on it and share their date.

The family calendar

Buried between dentist appointments

A birthday entry repeating every year works, until it's one line among forty and nobody is assigned to notice it.

One person's memory

Usually the same person, every year

In a lot of households one person quietly remembers for everyone. That works until they're busy, away, or tired of being the calendar.

A setup that works

Once

Collect every date in one go

Ask the family group chat for everyone's birthday, including nieces, nephews and in-laws. Add partners' parents. It takes ten minutes once and saves the scramble every year.

For each person

Pick the lead time, not just the date

Shipping a gift: a week or two ahead. A card in the mail: about a week. A call or a text: the morning of. The reminder should match what you actually do for that person.

For the house

Share the list

If you're a couple or a family, both people should see the same list. Then it doesn't matter who remembers; the list does.

For the big ones

Add the dates around birthdays

Anniversaries, Mother's Day (Sunday, May 9, 2027), Father's Day (Sunday, June 20, 2027), and the holidays your family celebrates. Same rule: remind early enough to act.

Test yourself

Without looking: when is your best friend's birthday? Your parents' anniversary? Take the 60-second "How Forgor are you?" test and see how many you'd have missed.
